## 2. Captains

- **2 captains, drawn at random** from the 16 — anyone can get the clipboard. Whoever's drawn is then randomly assigned to lead **North Hedges** or **South Hedges**.
- The captain's job: draft his 8-man roster, then declare and counter duos at both Pairings Nights. That's it — no other duties, no politics.
- **North Hedges and South Hedges are permanent — the rosters aren't.** The rivalry stays; who's on which side gets rebuilt from zero every year. No dynasties, no grudges that outlive the trip.

---

## 3. The Draft

Two captains, one snake draft, offline — same night as the fun round or before, commissioner's call. Full handicap info visible to both captains the whole time; this draft is about balance, not secrecy. Chris enters the two finished 8-man rosters into the app afterward. Nothing about this happens in-app — no boards, no timers, no reveal.

---

## 4. Pairings Night — the captain's game within the game

Twice per trip: **Friday night**, setting Saturday's matches, and **Saturday night**, setting Sunday's. Chris runs it live on a shared screen; every player's phone shows the same board updating in real time as picks land.

- **A coin flip, once, before Friday's Pairings Night**, decides who declares first and who counters first. **The winner chooses** — declaring shows your hand; countering means you pick knowing theirs.
- **Three rounds of declare-and-counter** set three matches: the declaring captain names one of his duos; the countering captain answers with a duo of his own to face them. That's a match, locked. Roles swap each round.
- **The fourth match sets itself.** After three rounds, each captain has exactly two players left — the board locks them in as Match 4 automatically.
- **Sunday flips the order** — whoever countered first on Friday declares first on Sunday. No second coin flip; the advantage balances itself across the weekend.
- Nothing says Friday's duos have to run it back Sunday. Reshuffle if you want to.

---

## 5. The competition

- **2-man scramble, gross, both days.** Both partners tee off; play the better ball; both play in from there. One team score per hole, no exceptions.
- Duo vs duo match play. Every match worth **3 points**: front 9, back 9, overall 18 (win = 1, halve = ½). 12 points per day, **24 total** — most cumulative points wins the cup.
- **No strokes given, anywhere.** Straight up, gross scramble, both rounds.
- **Handicaps are captain intel, not a scoring input.** Index shows on the Pairings Night board so captains know who they're matching up — beyond that, it does nothing.
- **The mercy rule — double bogey cap.** If a hole's not happening, pick it up. Worst it can cost your duo is a double bogey — a par 4 caps at 6, a par 5 at 7. The app enforces this in match scoring even if a bigger number gets written down.
- **The reverse mulligan — the duo's weapon.** Once per round, each duo can force the opposing duo to replay their last shot. Call it immediately, before the next shot — otherwise it's gone. Whatever they make on the replay is the score. No exceptions, no asterisks.

---

## 6. Drives Used

One tap per hole: whose tee shot the duo played. Eighteen taps a round, nothing more. It doesn't win or lose a hole — it settles the argument about who's actually carrying the team.

---

## 7. Money

- **The Challenge Ledger is the only money in this app.** Log a bet against anybody, any time — stake, plain-words terms, tap send. The other guy taps accept and it's official. Winner gets marked, the ledger updates, everyone can see it.
- **One-tap presets** for the trip's usual bets — no typing terms from scratch every time.
- No skins. No cup pot. The cup is played for the trophy and the champions wall, not cash.
- One settle-up number per man at trip's end.

---

## 8. Contingencies

- **Shortened event:** if Sunday can't be completed (departures, weather), the cup goes to the standings after the last fully completed round. Pre-decided — no bar-side rulings.
- **A tied cup goes to a chip-off.** Points, then total holes won across the trip, and if it's still dead even — two captains, one wedge each, on the practice green. Commissioner records the result. No co-champions.
- **Commissioner authority:** Chris resolves anything not covered here — score corrections, Challenge Ledger disputes, rule gaps. Rulings are final and entered through admin.
